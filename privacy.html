Privacy Policy

JSA Outreach · last updated 22 October 2026

JSA Outreach is a personal job-search tool built and used by Mohd Ashhar (Noida, India). It sends his job-application emails from his own Gmail account. It is not offered to anyone else, and only he can sign in to it. This policy explains what it does with Google account data and with the contact details of the people it emails.

Google user data it accesses

With the owner's consent, it accesses only his own Gmail account, through the Gmail API:

  • gmail.compose: to create drafts and send his job-application emails, with his resume attached.
  • gmail.readonly: to find replies to those emails, and bounce notices about them, in his mailbox.

It does not delete, label, archive or change any existing email.

How that data is used

  • Only to send the owner's emails, and to notice replies, bounces and opt-outs so that no one is emailed again after one of those.
  • Never for advertising, and never sold.
  • Never shared with AI services or used to train AI models.
  • No person other than the owner reads it.

What is stored, and where

The following are kept in a private database hosted by Supabase:

  • the OAuth tokens that keep the Gmail connection working;
  • for each email sent: the recipient addresses, subject, text, Gmail message and thread IDs, and the send time;
  • for each reply or bounce: its date and a short snippet (at most 500 characters), shown on the owner's private dashboard.

Only the owner's credentials can read the database. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by the provider.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with third parties. The service providers that run the tool for the owner process data only to provide their service:

  • Google, for Gmail;
  • Supabase, for storage and scheduled jobs.

If you received an email from JSA Outreach

To write to you, the tool used these business details: your name, job title, company, work email address, LinkedIn profile URL, and the job post that prompted the email. They came from public job posts (LinkedIn, Hacker News, remote job boards) and from SalesQL, a business contact-data provider.

Each person receives at most one email. Any reply, even one word, means you will never be emailed again.

To have your details deleted, write to the address below. One minimal "do not contact" entry is kept, so that the tool can never email you again.

Retention and deletion

  • Gmail tokens are kept until access is revoked.
  • Records of emails sent are kept, so that no one is ever emailed twice.
  • Contacts who were never emailed are deleted automatically after 90 days.

The owner can delete any of this at any time.

Revoking access

The Gmail connection can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

JSA Outreach's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
