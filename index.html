JSA Outreach

A personal job-search tool, built and used by Mohd Ashhar.

JSA Outreach helps one person apply for jobs: Mohd Ashhar, a full-stack AI engineer based in Noida, India. It finds people who are hiring for AI and full-stack engineering roles, from public job posts on LinkedIn, Hacker News "Who is hiring?" threads and remote job boards. It then sends each of them one short, personal email from his own Gmail account, with his resume attached.

It is not a public service. Nobody else can sign in to it or send email with it.

Google account access

With the owner's permission, JSA Outreach uses the Gmail API on his own Gmail account to:

  • create drafts and send his job-application emails (gmail.compose);
  • read replies to those emails and bounce notices about them (gmail.readonly). That way it never writes again to anyone who replied, whose address bounced, or who asked not to be contacted.
